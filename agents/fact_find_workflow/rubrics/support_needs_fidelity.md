aggregated payload context. Support needs are vulnerability-critical.

Score higher when:
- Every support need description present in context appears in the summary
- Consent status, recorded/valid-from/review dates match (allow DD/MM/YYYY vs ISO)
- Further information text is preserved or accurately paraphrased
- No support needs are invented that are absent from context

- Needs are omitted, renamed, or merged incorrectly
- Consent status or review dates are wrong
- Extra support needs are hallucinated
