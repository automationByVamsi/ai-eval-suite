## scenario

COMPLEX question (one page). A colleague is in the middle of a real case that does not fit the simple
path: the customer's situation has a condition, an exception, a deadline, or a second need. They
describe the situation and ask what to do. It is sent with question_type "how", so the agent answers
with the steps for that situation.
The agent treats a question like this (several asks, or joined by "and", "if", "when", "but", "also",
"while") as complex: it splits it into sub-questions, searches for each and combines the evidence. These
questions test that path, which is where a colleague most needs the agent and where a wrong answer costs
most (a missed deadline, a wrong refund, a vulnerable customer handled the standard way).

## task

Read the page for the points where the procedure CHANGES: a condition or threshold, an exception, an
escalation or referral, a time limit, a brand / channel / system difference, a customer circumstance
(vulnerability, bereavement, fraud, a complaint already open), or a step that must NOT be taken. Build
one realistic case around one or two of them and ask what to do. Vary across questions:
- a situation plus the ask ("customer is complaining that ..., what do I need to do?");
- a condition inside the task ("how do I ... when the customer has already ...");
- two linked asks the page answers together ("what's the process for ... and when do I escalate it?");
- the edge the page calls out ("we're out of time due to bank error and the customer has lost money,
  what steps do I take?");
- a case where the right answer is to stop, refuse, refer or escalate instead of carrying on.
Every condition in the question must be one the page covers, so this page alone settles the whole
answer.

## additional_guidance

Start from the customer's situation, not the procedure's name, the way the colleague would describe the
case to a team leader. One to three conditions, each of which changes the answer; leave out details that
change nothing. Never build a case that needs another page to answer. If the page has no condition,
exception or variation at all, write the most demanding realistic case its procedure supports instead.

## input_format

One or two sentences in UK English, colleague tone, 15-50 words: the situation, then the question.

## expected_output_format

Numbered steps for THIS situation, in the page's order, one action per step: the steps that apply under
the question's conditions, including the exception, escalation or referral path the page gives for them,
and leaving out branches that do not apply. Where the page says something must not be done, that is a
step ("Do not ...; instead ..."). Keep any warning, timescale or prerequisite the page attaches to a step
in that step. When the question has two asks, cover both (the steps, then a final step or two for the
second ask). Nothing that is not on the page; no introduction or closing sentence.
