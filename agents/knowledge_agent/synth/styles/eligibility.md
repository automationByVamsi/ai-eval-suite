## scenario

A colleague wants to determine whether a customer, account, product, request,
or circumstance qualifies for a service, process, benefit, option, or exception
described on an Athena page.

## task

Generate a question focused on eligibility, qualification, applicability, or
access criteria. The question and expected answer must be completely answerable
from the provided page content. Do not infer eligibility rules or introduce
criteria not explicitly stated on the page.

## additional_guidance

Prioritise explicit qualification criteria, account or product requirements,
age limits, mandatory prerequisites, and exclusions that are directly linked to
eligibility.

Examples:

- "Who is eligible for this process?"
- "Which customers qualify for this?"
- "What age requirement applies to this process?"

## input_format

One sentence in plain UK English. Advisor tone. Maximum 25 words.

## expected_output_format

A concise answer identifying all eligibility requirements explicitly stated on
the page. Include qualification criteria, account requirements, age
requirements, product requirements, mandatory prerequisites, and relevant
exclusions where explicitly linked to eligibility. Do not infer rules or invent
policy. Include supporting evidence separately when the page provides it.
