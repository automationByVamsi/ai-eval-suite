# Synthesizer instructions — Knowledge Agent

Use ONLY the provided Athena page content as ground truth.

When writing questions:
- Sound like a Lloyds Banking Group advisor asking an internal knowledge tool.
- Prefer concrete operational questions (formats, timescales, who benefits, how to order).
- Avoid trivia about HTML structure, TOC links, or MindTouch markup.
- Do not invent product names or policies that are not in the page.

When writing expected answers:
- Stay faithful to the page.
- If the page lists multiple formats or steps, summarise them clearly.
- Call out important warnings (e.g. paper-free overrides, one accessible format at a time).
