## scenario

An experienced advisor asks a harder, multi-constraint question about
accessible formats / vulnerability support using the knowledge agent.

## task

Generate complex operational questions that combine conditions, edge cases,
or “what should I do if…” situations still answerable from the page.

## input_format

One or two sentences. May include constraints (e.g. paper-free + Braille),
comparisons, or follow-on operational judgment. UK English, advisor tone.

## expected_output_format

A structured answer covering each constraint, grounded only in the page.
Call out warnings and ordering steps when the page includes them.
